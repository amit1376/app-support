About Raag SaReGaMa

Raag SaReGaMa is a music practice app for exploring Indian classical music through Raag, Taal and Saragam.

Explore the structure of a Raag, listen to its notes, understand rhythmic cycles, and practice Saragam with musical accompaniment.

Explore Indian Classical Music

Learn the structure of Raag and Taal while hearing the music rather than only reading about it.

01

Explore Raags

View information about Raag including Aroha, Avaroha, Pakad and example Saragam material.

02

Understand Taal

Explore rhythmic cycles through their matras, vibhags, Taali, Khaali and theka.

03

Practice Saragam

Play musical notes at a selected tempo and practice Saragam with rhythmic accompaniment.

Listen and Practice

The app is designed to make musical concepts audible.

Choose Your Tonic

Select the tonic that matches your preferred pitch, then hear the musical notes relative to that tonic.

Hear the Notes

Play individual notes and Saragam sequences using the app's instrument playback.

Practice with Rhythm

Saragam can be accompanied by the selected Taal, allowing the melody and rhythmic cycle to play together on a common musical timeline.

Musical Sound

The app uses real-time audio synthesis and playback to produce its musical sounds and rhythmic accompaniment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Raag SaReGaMa does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

Does the app require an internet connection?

The app's core music content and playback are designed to work on the device. An internet connection is not required for normal music playback.

Can I choose a different tonic?

Yes. The app supports selectable tonic options so that musical notes can be played relative to the pitch you prefer.

Where is my practice data stored?

App data is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account or a developer-operated cloud service for its core functionality.

Where can I find information about privacy?

Please see the Raag SaReGaMa Privacy Policy for information about how the app handles data.

Does the app use open-source software?

Yes. Raag SaReGaMa uses the Synthesis ToolKit (STK) for audio synthesis. See the Third-Party Software page for attribution and license information.

Need Help?

If you have a question, encounter a playback problem, or have a suggestion for improving Raag SaReGaMa, we'd be happy to hear from you.

When reporting a playback problem, please include the Raag or Saragam you were playing and, if relevant, the selected tonic and tempo.
