Privacy Policy

Raag SaReGaMa

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Raag SaReGaMa ("the App"), developed by Amit Khare, handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Raag SaReGaMa does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address as part of the App's core functionality.

The App does not use advertising or analytics services to track how you use the App.

Information Stored on Your Device

Raag SaReGaMa may store app content and your app-related information locally on your device so that the App can provide its features.

This may include locally stored music content, practice material, Saragam information, Taal information, and playback preferences.

This information is not uploaded to servers operated by the developer as part of the normal operation of the App.

Audio and Playback

The App generates and plays musical audio on your device. Audio playback does not require sending your musical practice or playback information to the developer.

The App uses the open-source Synthesis ToolKit (STK) for audio synthesis. STK is included as software within the application and does not require an account or transmit your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Raag SaReGaMa does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or cloud services to collect information about your use of the App.

The App may include third-party open-source software libraries used to provide its functionality. These libraries do not change the App's privacy practices described in this policy.

Information We Do Not Sell or Share

We do not sell your personal information or your practice-related information to third parties.

Because the App does not transmit this information to developer-operated servers during normal operation, we do not receive your locally stored practice information.

Data Security

Information stored by the App remains on your device. The security of that information therefore also depends on the security features of your device and operating system.

We recommend using the security features provided by your device, such as a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

Data Deletion

You can delete information using functionality provided by the App where applicable. Removing the App from your device may also remove its locally stored data, subject to your device and any backups you may have created.

Children's Privacy

Raag SaReGaMa is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Raag SaReGaMa or applicable privacy requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
