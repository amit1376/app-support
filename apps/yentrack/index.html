About YenTrack

YenTrack is a personal finance app designed to help you organize and understand your credit card spending.

Import your credit card statements, review your transactions, organize spending into categories, and understand your spending through summaries and reports.

Statement Import

YenTrack supports statement formats from selected credit card issuers.

Supported statement formats

Statement formats can differ between credit card issuers and may also change over time. YenTrack supports specific formats that have been configured for the app.

If your statement is not recognized or the imported information appears incorrect, please contact support with details about the credit card issuer and the problem you encountered.

Frequently Asked Questions

What statement files can YenTrack import?

YenTrack supports specific statement formats from supported credit card issuers. Support for additional formats may be added in future versions.

What happens when I import a statement?

YenTrack reads the statement and extracts information such as transaction dates, merchant names and amounts. You can review the imported information before it is added to your records.

Can I categorize my transactions?

Yes. YenTrack allows transactions to be organized into spending categories. Merchant rules can also help automatically categorize transactions.

Does YenTrack require an account?

No. YenTrack does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Where can I find information about privacy?

Please see the YenTrack Privacy Policy for information about how YenTrack handles your data.

Need Help?
