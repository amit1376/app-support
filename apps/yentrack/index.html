About YenTrack

YenTrack helps you organize and understand your credit card spending by bringing transactions from your credit card statements together in one place.

You can import supported credit card statements, organize transactions into categories, and review your spending through summaries and reports.

Getting Started

Add a Credit Card

Add your credit card to YenTrack and select the appropriate card issuer and card type.

Import a Statement

Import a supported statement file for your card. YenTrack reads the statement and prepares the transactions for you to review before they are added to your records.

Review Your Spending

Once transactions have been imported, YenTrack provides summaries and reports to help you understand your spending patterns.

Statement Import

YenTrack supports statement formats from selected credit card issuers. Supported formats may vary by issuer and statement type.

If your statement cannot be imported correctly, please contact support with details about the issuer and the problem you encountered.
