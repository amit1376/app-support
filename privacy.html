Privacy Policy

YenTrack

Effective date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YenTrack ("the App"), developed by Amit Khare, handles information when you use the App.

Information You Enter or Import

YenTrack allows you to enter information about your credit cards and import credit card statements. This information may include transaction dates, merchant names, transaction amounts, categories, and other information contained in your statements.

YenTrack uses this information to provide its core functionality, including organizing transactions, categorizing spending, and providing spending summaries and reports.

How Your Data Is Stored

YenTrack is designed to store your financial information locally on your device. Your imported statements and transaction information are not transmitted to servers operated by the developer as part of the normal operation of the App.

Statement Files

When you import a statement into YenTrack, the App processes the file to extract information needed to create transactions and statement records.

Statement files are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

YenTrack does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, credit card number, or other personal information for the purpose of providing the App's core functionality.

YenTrack does not sell your personal information or financial information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

YenTrack does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about your use of the App.

Data Security

Because your financial information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device and your operating system.

We recommend using the security features provided by your device, including a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

Data Deletion

You can delete information stored by YenTrack using the functionality provided within the App. You can also remove the App from your device to remove its locally stored data, subject to the behavior of your device and any backups you may have created.

Children's Privacy

YenTrack is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to YenTrack or applicable privacy requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
